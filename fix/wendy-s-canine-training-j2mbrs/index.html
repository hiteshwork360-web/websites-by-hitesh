The fix, ready to copy

About 10 minutes. You change nothing on your website or your mailbox.

TypeName / HostValueAction
TXT@v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~allAdd (new)

TTL: leave the default (1 hour is fine). Some hosts want the full name, e.g. _dmarc.wendyscaninetraining.com instead of _dmarc.

Where to click in Wix

  1. In your Wix dashboard open Settings → Domains, click the ⋯ menu next to wendyscaninetraining.com, choose Manage DNS Records, then + Add Record.
  2. For each row above: choose type TXT, paste the Name / Host and the Value, and save.
  3. Wait about an hour, then open the MXToolbox check again: it should show a pass.
