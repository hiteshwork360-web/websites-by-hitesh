The fix, ready to copy

About 10 minutes. You change nothing on your website or your mailbox.

TypeName / HostValueAction
TXT@v=spf1 include:secureserver.net include:_spf.google.com include:dc-aa8e722993._spfm.empireplumbingac.com ~allReplace all SPF records with this one

TTL: leave the default (1 hour is fine). Some hosts want the full name, e.g. _dmarc.empireplumbingac.com instead of _dmarc.

Where to click in GoDaddy

  1. Sign in to GoDaddy, open My Products, choose DNS next to empireplumbingac.com, then Add New Record.
  2. For each row above: choose type TXT, paste the Name / Host and the Value, and save.
  3. Wait about an hour, then open the MXToolbox check again: it should show a pass.

Also worth doing: turn on DKIM in Google Workspace (5 minutes)

  1. Open admin.google.com → Apps → Google Workspace → Gmail → Authenticate email.
  2. Click Generate new record, copy the TXT value and add it in your DNS with the name google._domainkey.
  3. Back in the Admin console, click Start authentication.
