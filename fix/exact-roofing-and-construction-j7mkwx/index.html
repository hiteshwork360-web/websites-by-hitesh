The fix, ready to copy

About 10 minutes. You change nothing on your website or your mailbox.

TypeName / HostValueAction
TXT_dmarcv=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:todd@xroofing.com;Add (new)

TTL: leave the default (1 hour is fine). Some hosts want the full name, e.g. _dmarc.xroofing.com instead of _dmarc.

Where to click

  1. Sign in where your domain's DNS is managed (your name servers are ns1.azmediaproduction.com), open the DNS settings for xroofing.com, then add a new record.
  2. For each row above: choose type TXT, paste the Name / Host and the Value, and save.
  3. Wait about an hour, then open the MXToolbox check again: it should show a pass.

Also worth doing: turn on DKIM in Microsoft 365 (5 minutes)

  1. Open security.microsoft.com → Email & collaboration → Policies & rules → Threat policies → Email authentication settings → DKIM.
  2. Select xroofing.com; Microsoft shows two CNAME records (selector1._domainkey and selector2._domainkey). Add both in your DNS.
  3. Come back and switch on Sign messages for this domain with DKIM signatures.

The DMARC record above starts in safe monitoring mode (p=none): nothing about your own email changes. Daily reports will arrive at todd@xroofing.com.
